How to use this map

This is a big-picture atlas of artificial intelligence — from the mathematics and statistics underneath, through classical ML, deep learning, vision, NLP, reinforcement learning, generative models, the systems that ship them, and the responsible-AI layer around them. The goal is to let you see the whole territory at once and mark what you know vs. what you don't.

  • Mark familiarity on any topic — Unknown, Learning, Known. The map lights up as you go; your progress is saved in this browser.
  • Map view — the full landscape as a radial graph. Each colored sector is a domain; rings go domain → subdomain → topic. Drag to pan, scroll to zoom, hover to trace prerequisite/related links, click for detail.
  • Outline view — the same knowledge as a checklist with progress bars, ideal for quickly marking areas you already command (use the per-section bulk buttons).
  • Relationships — arrows point from a prerequisite to what it enables; dashed lines mark closely related topics. Use them to find the on-ramp into something unfamiliar.
  • Export / Import your progress as JSON to back it up or move browsers.
